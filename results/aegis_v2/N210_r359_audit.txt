N210.3 law: slip_m = mu_realized*Fn/KP; measured/predicted = 0.72-1.11 per (arm, suite) cell and median 1.02 per episode over 180 Coulomb-regime episodes (Fn >= 2 N), plus a 2.6 mm additive floor below it. Cause: the only tangential drive is kp*e and Coulomb transmits at most mu*Fn, so the steady sliding residual IS mu*Fn/kp. Corollary: holding e <= 25 mm at the top of the realized band needs KP >= 288 N/m at 10 N and 720 N/m at 25 N -- 12x and 29x the frozen KP=25.
