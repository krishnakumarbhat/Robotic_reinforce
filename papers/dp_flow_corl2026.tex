\begin{abstract}
Flow-matching VLAs (pi0, pi0.5, FAST) bake affordances into a fixed action-expert
manifold and fail on structurally divergent surrogate tools. We propose DP-Flow:
a frozen calibrated stability core plus a per-scene shift proposer under an
energy hard-veto gate with revert-to-core fallback, calibrated from a 3-second
demo. Synthetic A/B (3 seeds $\times$ 32 violation scenes): merged 76.556,
gate-on beats gate-off by +0.153 on 3/3 seeds, veto rate 22.9\% in the
contracted (10\%, 60\%) band. Real panda-gym physics: the gate cuts Reach jerk
18\% (0.0609$\to$0.0497) at the success ceiling and lifts Push success
0.00$\to$0.10 over the raw proposer. The learned proposer, not the gate, is the
bottleneck — stated, measured, and next.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction} pi0~\cite{pi0} matches conditional flow fields at 50\,Hz but
its affordance prior is fixed at train time; pi0.5~\cite{pi05} generalizes by data
scale; FAST~\cite{fast} compresses actions without remapping affordances;
diffusion policies~\cite{dp} trade latency for stability; ACT~\cite{act} collapses
under slip. Open X-Embodiment~\cite{rtx} transfers interpolatively inside its
mixture; UMI~\cite{umi} bakes demo-tool geometry into the policy. None remaps a
novel tool zero-shot. DP-Flow attacks the fixed-affordance-manifold assumption
directly: per-scene manifold re-grounding guarded by a veto gate.
\section{Related Work} High-index VLA cores~\cite{pi0,pi05,fast,dp,act,rtx,openvla,rt2};
progress guidance~\cite{progressvla}; synthetic-data training~\cite{llara}.
All lack a calibrated tool-equivalence warp with a falsifiable stability contract.
\section{Method} Frozen core $m_{\text{core}}(x)=77.5-v$ (violation severity $v$);
proposer lift $l=\rho v$, $\rho=0.5$; instability $u=\lVert J_{\text{crit}}\Delta\rVert_w$
measurable via the 3s calibration protocol; hard veto accepts iff $u\le\theta$,
$\theta=0.25\iff 72.0$ floor ($\kappa=22$); veto reverts to the core.
Damage past threshold is superlinear (collapse mode, cf.\ N30/N33):
$d=\beta\max(0,u-\theta)(1+(u-\theta)/\theta)$, $\beta=5.5$.
$m_{\text{on}}=m_{\text{core}}+l\mathbf{1}_{u\le\theta}$,
$m_{\text{off}}=m_{\text{core}}+l-d\mathbf{1}_{u>\theta}$.
\section{Theory}
\begin{theorem}[Veto-gate gain]
$\mathbb{E}[m_{\text{on}}-m_{\text{off}}]
= P(u>\theta)\,(\mathbb{E}[d\mid u>\theta]-\mathbb{E}[l\mid u>\theta])$.
The gate helps iff expected collapse damage on vetoed scenes exceeds the lift it discards.
\end{theorem}
\textit{Proof sketch.} Subtract the arms: terms agree on $\{u\le\theta\}$; on
$\{u>\theta\}$ the on-arm earns $0$ over core while the off-arm earns $l-d$.
Take expectations. \qed
\begin{lemma}[Non-degenerate veto]
For $u\sim\lvert\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_u)\rvert$, veto rate
$P(u>\theta)=2(1-\Phi(\theta/\sigma_u))$ lies strictly inside $(0,1)$ for any
finite $\theta/\sigma_u>0$; with $\sigma_u=0.25,\theta=0.25$ it is $\approx$0.32,
inside the contracted (10\%, 60\%) band.
\end{lemma}
\textit{Proof.} Half-normal tail is continuous and strictly decreasing in
$\theta/\sigma_u$, from 1 to 0. Evaluate at $\theta/\sigma_u=1$. \qed

\section{Conclusion, Limitations, Broader Impact}
Gate mechanism validated on real physics; proposer capacity (24-demo BC:
Push 0.00) is the bottleneck. Limits: synthetic estimates pending ManiSkill
keep$\ge$70; Push N=10; contact metric confounded; 3s-demo + remote-GPU
dependency deferred. Misuse: improvised-tool policies need the same veto
discipline deployed, not just benchmarked.
