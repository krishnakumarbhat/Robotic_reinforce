\section*{Claim 1: Standard LIBERO is saturated AND misleading}
Standard LIBERO: pi0.5 96.85\%, GR00T-N1.7 97.0\% (3B, paper-scale budgets).
LIBERO-PRO (May 2026): all SOTA collapse to $\approx$0\% under
position/instruction perturbations. Benchmark memorization, not competence.

\section*{Claim 2: Single-run LIBERO numbers are lottery tickets}
OUR measurement (SmolVLA-450M fine-tuned, 15k steps, local RTX 3050):
same task + same seed $\to$ 3/6 successes across 6 repetitions.
Three full 30-episode sweeps, identical seeds: 7/30, 8/30, 0/30-manual-loop.
Success flips run-to-run (unseeded flow-matching sampling + GPU nondeterminism).
Implication: report distributions, never single runs.

\section*{Claim 3: Action jerk predicts failure (dense signal where LIBERO is sparse)}
LIBERO reward is binary; failed episodes show competent reach-grasp-lift
with reward 0 throughout. Command-jitter energy separates outcomes:
\begin{itemize}
\item successes: mean jerk $0.155\pm0.025$, max jerk $0.651\pm0.168$
\item failures: mean jerk $0.180\pm0.045$, max jerk $0.980\pm0.263$
\item veto rule max\_jerk$>0.618$: precision 0.84, recall 1.00, F1 0.91 (n=30)
\item validation on held-out seeds: PENDING (seed-8000 run)
\end{itemize}
Combined with a scripted fallback that never scores 0 (measured adapt gap
$-0.125$ under novelty where learned policies score 0.06), the gated system
has higher expected real-world success than a 0.97$\to$0.0 collapser.

\section*{Claim 4: Efficiency axis (uncontested)}

$\to$ 0.368 (15k$\times$b8) on PushT; LIBERO-spatial 35\% probe, 23.3\%
